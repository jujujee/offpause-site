What information we use

Where we obtain information

For our own business development, we may obtain business contact information from an organisation’s own website, Companies House or other appropriate current public business sources.

For client campaigns, the relevant cleaning company supplies or instructs us to process its own selected prior enquiry and quotation information. We may also receive information directly from you during calls, emails, contracts, payments or privacy correspondence.

When providing information is necessary

If you are a sole trader or individual client and we need particular contact, contract or billing information to take requested pre-contract steps or to perform an agreement, not providing it may mean we cannot enter into or administer the service. Some business and accounting information may also need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

If you receive marketing from Offpause, you are not required to give us any additional personal information merely because we have contacted you.

Direct marketing and your right to object

We use targeted rather than indiscriminate outreach. Before live marketing calls we apply the relevant telephone preference and suppression checks under our screening procedure. We identify Offpause, display a callback number, and stop the affected marketing when you object.

You can tell us at any time that you do not want direct marketing from Offpause, and we will stop. We may keep the minimum information necessary — usually the telephone number, the date and the scope of your objection — so that we do not contact you again by mistake. That information is never used to target you.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for its purpose and for our legal and accountability needs. The periods below are the defaults and criteria we have adopted. They are not universal statutory periods.

  • Our own business development. Closed or unresponsive contacts are normally removed or minimised from our active CRM within 30 days after the contact sequence closes. Genuine active conversations and agreed follow-ups are reviewed at least every six months.
  • Client campaign working information. Raw candidate files are intended to be short-lived. Selected campaign working information is normally removed no later than 30 days after the final report is issued, unless the client asks for a compatible shorter period, or a documented complaint, dispute, legal hold or other justified purpose requires limited retention.
  • Screening evidence. Detailed screening evidence may be kept for up to 12 months, but only where the accepted campaign retention arrangement provides for it, or where we have a separately documented lawful reason to keep a minimum accountability record. Where that period applies it runs from the last relevant call or the closure of the campaign, using the trigger recorded for that campaign; the trigger and expiry date are recorded at the time and are never chosen after the event. This does not allow us to keep a whole prospect file or detailed call notes.
  • Objections. Minimum suppression information may be kept for as long as it is reasonably needed to honour an objection, so that we do not contact you again by mistake.
  • Contracts, invoices and commercial evidence. Normally about six years after the engagement ends, subject to applicable tax and legal requirements. Unnecessary prospect-level details are minimised sooner.
  • Complaint, rights-request and incident logs. Normally three years after the matter closes, with unnecessary supporting personal detail removed sooner. A continuing matter or a justified hold may require limited longer retention.

Where we hold information only on a cleaning company’s instructions, that company’s accepted retention instructions govern it, except where we have a separate lawful reason to keep a narrowly defined record of our own.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and the lawful basis, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, data portability. You also have rights concerning certain solely automated decisions; Offpause does not use solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects in this service. Where we rely on consent for an activity, you may withdraw it.

You always have the right to object to direct marketing, and we will stop the affected marketing when you do.

To exercise any right, contact privacy@offpause.co.uk. If your request relates to information we process only on behalf of a cleaning company, we may need to pass it to that company or assist them as the controller — but we will not use that distinction as a reason to ignore your request.

Data protection complaints

You can complain to complaints@offpause.co.uk. We acknowledge a data protection complaint within the applicable 30-day maximum — sooner wherever we can — investigate it appropriately without undue delay, and tell you the outcome.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Their current guidance on how to do that is at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Security and automated decisions

We use access controls, business accounts, restricted access to the devices and accounts we work from, number screening, and deletion and suppression procedures appropriate to this service. Call recording is off. We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
