Offpause

A commercial quote going quiet doesn’t always mean the opportunity disappeared.

Offpause is a UK service for commercial cleaning companies. We take an agreed candidate batch of your older enquiries and quotations, screen it, confirm the final calling list with you, and then work the approved records.

The job is to find out what actually happened: whether there is a current or upcoming requirement, what has changed since, and who is involved in the decision now. Where there is a genuine need that fits what you do, we arrange the next step with your team — a callback, a quotation discussion or a site survey.

You price and close. We provide the call-attempt and outcome records, prompt handovers and a final record-level report.

Got older commercial quotes sitting untouched?

Tell us roughly what your backlog looks like and we’ll see whether a small reactivation test makes sense.
