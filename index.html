Offpause

Old commercial quotes don’t stop being worth something just because the follow-up stopped.

Offpause is a UK service for commercial cleaning companies. We take a selected batch of your older enquiries and quotations, screen them, and call them.

The job is to find out what actually happened: whether there is a current or upcoming requirement, what has changed since, and who is involved in the decision now. Where there is a genuine need that fits what you do, we arrange the next step with your team — a callback, a quotation discussion or a site survey.

You price and close. We provide the calling record, the handovers and a report on every record we worked.
